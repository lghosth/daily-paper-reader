## 摘要
预训练语言模型需要巨大的计算量，而合适的优化器可以节省其中相当一部分。Muon 的谱步比符号步提供更强的方向，但代价高昂：每一步都要在全矩阵上运行 Newton-Schulz 迭代，并且在分布式训练中还要额外进行一次 all-reduce。如 Lion 和 Signum 中的符号步则计算便宜，且可保持在各设备本地。我们提出 LionMuon：每 $P$ 次迭代执行一次 Muon 步，其间执行 Lion 步，二者共享单个双 EMA 动量缓冲区。Muon 的计算和通信每 $P$ 步才支付一次，优化器状态仅为 AdamW 的一半。单 EMA 变体 SignMuon 已经优于 Muon。我们证明了重尾噪声下的复杂度界，其中周期在 Muon 与 Lion 的光滑性常数和噪声常数之间形成插值，并说明了 LionMuon 何时比两者都快。在 FineWeb 上训练的 124M 和 355M 模型上，$P=2$ 和 $P=5$ 的 LionMuon 在相同 token 数下达到比 Muon、AdamW、Lion 和 Signum 更低的损失。在 4-GPU 数据并行训练下，它在 PCIe 上少用三分之一的墙钟时间即可达到 Muon 的最终损失，并且在不增加暴露通信的情况下，在损失上优于通信高效的 Muon 变体 Dion 和 MuonBP，同时保持精确梯度。代码：https://github.com/brain-lab-research/lion-muon

## Abstract
Pretraining a language model takes enormous compute, and the right optimizer can save a good part of it. Muon's spectral step gives a stronger direction than a sign step, but it is expensive. Every step runs Newton-Schulz iterations on the full matrix and, in distributed training, an extra all-reduce. Sign steps, as in Lion and Signum, are cheap and stay local to each device. We propose LionMuon, which takes one Muon step every $P$ iterations and Lion steps in between, with a single dual-EMA momentum buffer shared by both. Muon's compute and communication are paid once per $P$ steps, and the optimizer state is half of AdamW's. A single-EMA variant, SignMuon, already improves on Muon. We prove complexity bounds under heavy-tailed noise in which the period sets an interpolation between Muon's and Lion's smoothness and noise constants, and which say when LionMuon is faster than both. On 124M and 355M models trained on FineWeb, LionMuon with $P=2$ and $P=5$ reaches a lower loss than Muon, AdamW, Lion and Signum at the same number of tokens. Under 4-GPU data-parallel training it reaches Muon's final loss with a third less wall-clock on PCIe, and it beats the communication-efficient Muon variants Dion and MuonBP on loss at no more exposed communication, while keeping the exact gradient.
